# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work and not the
person, and no harassment of any kind.

Report problems privately through GitHub's **Security -> Report a
vulnerability** form. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and
may ban people who break these rules.
