# Privacy

Clawd Music collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

- No telemetry, analytics, cookies or accounts.
- It stores on your computer only your volume, mute state, last station,
  glyph choice and minute totals, in the plugin store Claude Code provides.
  `/music reset` clears the station and volume; removing the plugin removes
  the rest.
- Playing a station connects your machine to that station's server (and to
  YouTube for YouTube stations). Those operators see your IP address as they
  would for any radio stream. Clawd Music never sees or stores that traffic.
